# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in QueryX a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical preference.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and explain project constraints without gatekeeping.
- Keep reviews about the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or private data.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about project scope and safety boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, publishing private information, or retaliation are not tolerated in project spaces or when representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [uulab.official@gmail.com](mailto:uulab.official@gmail.com). Maintainers will review reports promptly, preserve confidentiality where possible, and may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently ban a participant. Good-faith reports will not result in retaliation.

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. Decisions may consider context, impact, repetition, and the safety of affected community members.

## Scope

This policy applies in repositories, issue trackers, code reviews, project communication, events, and public representation of QueryX.

This code is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
